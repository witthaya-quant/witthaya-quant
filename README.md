I have worked in financial markets since 2012, mainly across proprietary trading, market making and trading-related businesses. Over time, that work has also brought me into financial product development and the technology behind market and investment workflows.

My main focus is trading and investment businesses: how markets function, how strategies behave, how risk and capital are managed, and how the surrounding business and technology need to work together. I am also interested in the broader design of financial products and systems.

I usually work between the financial or business problem and the people building the solution. That means understanding the mechanics first, setting product or business direction, making requirements clear, and working with domain specialists and engineers on implementation. Software is part of the work, but my contribution is not limited to writing code.

## How I approach problems

I prefer to understand how a market, business or workflow actually operates before choosing a model or a technology. I make assumptions explicit, look at trade-offs, test what can be tested, and distinguish what the evidence shows from what still needs to be established.

I value clear reasoning, practical outcomes and honest treatment of limitations. A useful system is not defined by how complicated it looks, but by whether it addresses the right problem and works under its real constraints.
